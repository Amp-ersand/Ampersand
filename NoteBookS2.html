Materials

-Pattern.

-Less than one metre of not streachy fabric. (choose two colours if you want a different inside and outside)

-Paper and glue (PVA or modpodge) to make a notebook or a pre-fabricated notebook of the correct size.

-A4 paper. (seperate from pattern paper for interfaceing)

-Two elastics the width of your notebook.

-Sewing Supplies.

-Optionial carabiner

Pattern Download

Written Instructions;

(Video Instructions coming soon)

Step 1 - Assemble pattern and cut pattern peices.

Step 2 - Glue and cut note book. (glue several pages together at the back or use cardstock for stability) Skip this step if you have a pre-fab notebook.

Step 3 - Sew Carabiner loop to outter fabric on the good side.

Step 4 - Place and pin pattern peices wrong sides together with elastics and book pocket sandwiched between.

Step 5 - Sew pattern peices together leaving a gap to turn out.

Step 6 - Cut excess and zigzag raw edge.

Step 7 - Turn right side out.

Step 8 - Iron flat and place folded A4 paper inside.

Step 9 - Quilt down everything.

Step 10 - Add your notebook.
