Materials

-One T-shirt or similar amount in other fabric.

-Less than one metre of non-streachy fabric.

-Four Large D-Rings.

-Draw string or aditional fabric to make one.

-Sewing Supplies.

-Optionial carabiner

Pattern Download

Written Instructions;

(Video Instructions coming soon)

Step 1 - Assemble pattern and cut pattern peices. ('Bag' peice can be cut from the sleeve of a large T-shirt)

Step 2 - Pin lanyard peice, good sides together along the length. (Also sew your bag peice if you are not useing a T-shirt)

Step 3 - Sew the length, keeping a uniform and straight stitch.

Step 4 - Cut the excess and zigzag stitch the raw edge.

Step 5 - Turn right side out.

Step 6 - Iron flat and sew long edges.

Step 7 - Attach lanyard peice to bag peice making loops for D-Rings and optional carabiner. (*Tip* use a zipper foot to get tight to the D-Ring.)

Step 8 - Sew together lining peices and attach at mouth of the bag.

Step 9 - Sew the bottom of the bag closed from the inside.

Step 10 - Sew closed the lining.

Step 11 - Create hole in bag mouth to create a channel and thread drawstring. (use a large safety pin)
