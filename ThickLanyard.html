Materials

-One long strip of fabric or Polo shirt as long as you want your lanyard + 5cm and 3 times a wide as your D-rings. (use the buttons of an old polo shirt as a lipbalm pocket)

-Two Large D-Rings

-Sewing Supplies

-Optionial carabiner

Written Instructions;

(Video Instructions coming soon)

Step 1 - Fold your fabric strip long ways and pin good sides together.

Step 2 - Sew the length, keeping a uniform and straight stitch.

Step 3 - Cut the excess and zigzag stitch the raw edge.

Step 4 - Turn right side out.

Step 5 - Iron flat and sew long edges. (Sew perpendicular at bottom of pocket and do not sew past)

Step 6 - Attach loop for carabeiner.

Step 7 - attach loop for D-Rings and sew down (*Tip* use a zipper foot to get tight to the D-Ring.)
